In a quiet bright garden lived a little rose bud named Rosie. She was small and wrapped up tight in her green leaves. Every day she looked across the garden to the shiny pond.
There lived Flora the lotus. Flora had big pink petals that opened perfectly every morning upon the quiet water. Rosie wanted to be just like her but her own petals felt stuck.
I will never be as pretty or as brave as Flora thought Rosie. She felt very sad and wanted to stay hidden forever in her tight little green shell.
But the garden loved Rosie and wanted to see her bloom. The breezy wind gently tickled her leaves to wake her up.The kind rain dropped cool water on her roots so she could drink and grow strong. Ray the bright sun wrapped her in a warm golden hug.
They all told Rosie that she was safe and it was time to show her colors. But Rosie was still scared to open up. She thought she was not good enough.
Then came the bugs. Buzz the fluffy bumblebee bumped into her stem. He was clumsy but very friendly. He told Rosie that the garden was missing her special red color.
 Next Honey the little honeybee flew near her leaves.
Honey explained that they needed her sweet pollen to make the best honey in the world.
Flora the lotus is beautiful Honey said but she grows in the water. We need you here on the dry land.
Rosie listened to her friends. She realized that she did not need to be a lotus at all.
She was meant to be a rose. She had her own special magic and the garden needed her exactly the way she was.
With a strong and happy heart Rosie pushed her petals outward. She stretched and stretched until she finally popped open. She was a beautiful velvet red rose.
Pip cheered by blowing a happy breeze. Plop made a beautiful rainbow in the sky.
Ray shined brighter than ever.Buzz and Honey danced around her soft sweet petals.
Rosie looked at the pond and smiled at Flora. They were both different and they were both perfect.
The Lesson: You do not need to be like anyone else to be amazing. You have your own special gifts and when you believe in yourself you will bloom beautifully in your own perfect time.
