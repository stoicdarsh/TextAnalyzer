Deep in the heart of the bright green jungle everything was perfectly still. Caspian the lion king with a shiny golden mane was shivering inside his cold cave. He used to be the biggest and strongest animal in the forest but a bad fever had made his legs wobbly and taken away his loud roar. Now he just looked small and tired.
For a long time Caspian had been a very mean king. He hunted constantly and almost every animal in the jungle like the monkeys the deer and the wild pigs was scared of him. When the wind carried the news that the scary lion was sick the animals were happy at first. They thought they were finally safe.
But after a few days the animals started to feel bad. Even though Caspian was scary nobody liked seeing someone hurt and all alone.
One evening the animals had a meeting by the big river. Elowen a wise and gentle old elephant told everyone that if they just watch him be sick when they know how to help it makes their own hearts dark and sad.
So the animals did something incredibly brave.
A little bunny named Briar was the first to step forward. She was terrified of Caspian but she carefully hopped into his cave and dropped soft healing leaves right next to his big paws. Next the monkeys swung down from the tall trees bringing coconut shells filled with cool fresh water. Bright colorful birds flew in and sang sweet happy songs to help the lion fall asleep.
Caspian opened his heavy eyes and watched them. He was so confused. When his scratchy voice finally came back he asked the little bunny why she was helping him when he had always been so mean.
Briar took a tiny step back but she stood tall and brave. She told him that if they leave him there to be sick and sad they become mean too. She said they choose to help because they are kind.
Weeks later Caspian finally walked out of his cave. He moved a little slower and his eyes looked much softer. The whole jungle held its breath waiting to see what he would do. But the great lion did not roar or chase them. Instead he bowed his big golden head to the animals silently promising to be a kind and gentle king from now on.
The Lesson: Being kind to someone who has been mean to you is one of the bravest things you can do. Choosing to help others even when it is hard makes the world a better place and fills your own heart with joy.
